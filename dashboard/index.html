Which pre-token protocol is worth farming next?

Every protocol in the sealed snapshot that records no token yet, ranked by what its launch would be worth if its record stayed as it is. Implied value is this radar’s own claim, never a prediction that a token will launch, or when.

How the value is computed

implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth multiplier × maturity multiplier

The category multiple is the market’s own pricing: the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of live-token protocols in the same category (at least 4 of them), otherwise the global median, winsorised to 0.05–0.80. Growth is the 7-day TVL change, clamped to −60%…+120%, at half weight. Maturity rises from 0.60 to 1.00 over a protocol’s first three years with no token. No protocol name is ever special-cased: the same formula scores every row.

Read this before you farm

  • “Records no token” means the snapshot’s DefiLlama record carries no token for the protocol. It is not proof that none exists: a protocol on this board may still have a token its record does not carry.
  • Wrapped, staked and bridged products of tokens that already trade are left off the board entirely: they are not launches still to come.
  • Implied value is a ranking device, not a price target and not financial advice. Never pay to claim an airdrop, and never connect a wallet holding funds you cannot afford to lose.
  • Data: sealed DefiLlama snapshot, protocols at or above $5,000,000 TVL. The watchlist floor is $50,000,000 TVL. Farm plans are generated from each protocol’s own category, chains, TVL and age.
